About Us

Building Digital Platforms for Communities and Society

Kartikey Tech Services is an enterprise-oriented technology and social development organization engaged in building practical digital solutions and services across areas including consultancy, research & development, platform design, technology implementation, and production-oriented digital initiatives.

One of its major social-platform initiatives is AllSamajVivah Control Center — a broader matrimonial technology framework designed to serve diverse societies and communities irrespective of religion, caste, region, or other community-specific structures.

The AllSamajVivah framework is designed so that individual communities can operate matrimonial platforms aligned with their own identity, traditions, preferences, and community requirements while benefiting from a common technology and governance foundation.

SainiSamajVivah is the currently operational community matrimonial platform under this framework, serving members of the Saini, Mali, Shakya, Kushwah and Maurya communities through a structured digital matrimonial service.

The broader vision of AllSamajVivah is to progressively support additional community-specific matrimonial platforms, including intended future services such as BrahminSamajVivah and VaishySamajVivah, while maintaining appropriate privacy, platform governance, community configuration, and technology standards.

SainiSamajVivah is operated by Kartikey Tech Services under the AllSamajVivah Control Center framework.
